# Big Rock: Partner Planning (Quota & Territory)

## Objective

Annual partner quota-setting and territory design grounded in attribution and
scorecard data instead of negotiation theater. The end state: a repeatable
planning motion where quota scenarios can be generated, stress-tested, and
defended with the same data partners are measured on.

This rock is deliberately still `planned` — it shows the front of the
lifecycle. Its first skill exists only as a proposal in the Improvement Log
below; per the operating model, skills are proposed in a rock's plan before
they are built.

## Milestones

- [ ] Define quota inputs: trailing attributed revenue, sourced pipeline, segment capacity
- [ ] Draft `/quota-scenario` skill (proposed in 2026-06-05 retro): generate quota scenarios under different growth assumptions
- [ ] Territory overlap analysis: flag partners competing for the same segment
- [ ] Dry-run FY28 planning cycle on synthetic data

## Owned Skills

None yet. `/quota-scenario` is proposed (see Improvement Log) and will be
built when this rock goes `active`.

## Open Questions

- Quota basis: trailing-twelve-month attributed revenue, or sourced pipeline
  with a conversion assumption?
- Should territory design reuse the guiding-principles approach (customer
  continuity, account proximity, industry mix) from prior GTM work?
